About Zaitoon

Gather around the table

Zaitoon Family Restaurant is a full‑service dining and events venue on Main G.T. Road, Wah Cantt. Our menu brings together Thai, Chinese, Continental and Pakistani cooking — from wok‑tossed Szechuan chicken to karahi finished in desi ghee — served in a warm family hall and on an open‑air rooftop under string lights.

Whether it's a weeknight dinner, a birthday, or a family event, we're set up for groups of every size, with dining packages to match every budget.
